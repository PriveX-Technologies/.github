## ⚡ Who We Are

**Privex Technologies** builds **custom software, AI systems, IoT and embedded hardware, and proprietary products** — engineered for the real world, with a strong emphasis on **security, performance, and scalability**.

We don't chase hype. We engineer things that **work** — from edge devices and inference pipelines to mixed-reality interfaces and secure platform infrastructure.
